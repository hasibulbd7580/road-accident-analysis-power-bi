# 🚗 Road Accident Analysis — Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing road accident data from **2019–2022**.

The project explores accident trends, severity, casualties, road and environmental conditions, vehicle involvement, and geographic patterns.

## 🔎 Key Insights

* Recorded accidents decreased by approximately **20.7% from 2019 to 2022**.
* Rural accidents had a higher fatal-accident percentage than urban accidents (**2.34% vs 0.73%**).
* Darkness without lighting had a higher fatal-accident percentage than daylight (**4.31% vs 1.05%**).
* Cars represented approximately **75.38%** of accident records.
* Average casualties increased as the number of vehicles involved increased.
* Single carriageways accounted for approximately **74.49%** of recorded accidents.
* November had the highest monthly accident count.

## ⚠️ Limitations

* Accident counts do not represent risk without traffic-exposure data.
* Geographic comparisons do not account for population or traffic volume.
* Correlation does not imply causation.
* Some categories contain missing or unknown values.
